The Curriculum

Here's what you'll cover,
topic by topic.

Not random YouTube playlists. A structured path from the basics to the stuff that actually trips people up, margins collapsing, the box model, specificity. Each topic has video lessons and a quiz you need to pass before moving on.

01

CSS Introduction

What CSS actually is, how it connects to HTML, and writing your first stylesheet. No fluff.

02

Syntax Deep Dive

Selectors, combinators, pseudo-elements, the stuff you need to actually target what you want to style.

03

Colors

RGB, HEX, HSL, understand the difference and when to use each. Stop copy-pasting color codes blindly.

04

Backgrounds

Background images, gradients, positioning. The visual foundation of every page you'll ever build.

05

Borders

Border styles, widths, rounded corners. Turns out border-radius: 50% isn't the only thing you need.

06

Margins & the Box Model

This is where most beginners get stuck. Content, padding, border, margin, learn why your layout keeps breaking.

07

Padding & Outline

Inner spacing and outlines. The other half of the box model puzzle.

08

Text

Alignment, decoration, spacing, transforms. Making text look good is harder than people think.

09

Fonts

Font families, Google Fonts, font-size, weight, style. Typography sets the tone of your whole site.

10

Links, Lists & Tables

Styling navigation, ordered/unordered lists, and making tables not look like it's 2005.
